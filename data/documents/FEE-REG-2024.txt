TUITION FEE SCHEDULE AND REFUND REGULATIONS (v3.0)
Document ID: FEE-REG-2024
Authority Level: 1 (Regulations)
Issuer: Finance & Accounts Committee
Effective Date: 2024-07-01
Scope: All University Students
================================================================================

SECTION 1: FEE STRUCTURE AND PAYMENT
1.1 Tuition fees and institutional charges must be settled prior to the start of instruction each semester.

SECTION 2: DUE DATES AND LATE PENALTIES
2.1 Payment Deadline: Full semester fees must be paid within 15 calendar days from the opening date of the semester.
2.2 Late Surcharge: A late fee of Rs. 100 per day is levied from day 16 to day 30. Thereafter, student registration is frozen.

SECTION 4: REFUND REGULATIONS
4.1 Withdrawal before Commencement: 100% refund of tuition fee minus Rs. 1,000 administrative charge.
4.2 Withdrawal within 15 Days of Orientation: 80% tuition fee refund.
4.3 Withdrawal after 30 Days: No tuition fees will be refunded under any circumstances.
