SECTION 1: ENACTMENT
1.1 These regulations govern undergraduate B.Tech cohorts enrolled between 2021 and June 2024.

SECTION 4: ATTENDANCE RULES (HISTORICAL)
4.1 Minimum Attendance Requirement: Every student must maintain at least 70.0% attendance in each registered course to be permitted to sit for the end-semester examinations.
4.2 Condonation: Condonation of 5% was permitted on medical grounds.

SECTION 8: PASSING MARKS
8.1 Passing Requirement: 40.0% aggregate marks required to clear a course.
