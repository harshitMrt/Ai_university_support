SECTION 1: LAB ATTENDANCE AND SUBMISSIONS
1.1 Lab Practical Attendance: In line with departmental standards, students enrolled in CS courses (CS201, CS202, CS203) are expected to attend all scheduled laboratory sessions.
1.2 Missed Experiments: Any experiment missed due to certified medical reasons must be performed and evaluated during the designated Make-Up Saturday sessions within 14 days.
1.3 Hardware Safety: No food, beverages, or unauthorized USB storage media are permitted inside the computing laboratories.

SECTION 2: LAB ATTENDANCE POLICY
2.1 Practical Threshold: Students in B.Tech CSE must maintain a mandatory minimum of 80.0% practical attendance in all computer science laboratory courses.
