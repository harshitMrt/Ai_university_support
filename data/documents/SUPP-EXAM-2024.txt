SECTION 1: OBJECTIVE
1.1 Special supplementary examinations provide an opportunity for students who failed regular courses to clear backlogs without losing an academic year.

SECTION 2: ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA FOR SUPPLEMENTARY EXAM
2.1 Attendance Prerequisite: A student MUST have maintained a minimum of 75.0% attendance during the regular semester offering of the course. A student who received an 'AD' (Attendance Debarred) grade is strictly NOT eligible for supplementary exams and must repeat the course.
2.2 Backlog Count Limit: A student must have no more than 2 active backlogs at the time of application. Students with 3 or more active backlogs are not permitted to register for the immediate supplementary session.
2.3 Result Status: The student must have received grade 'F' in the immediate preceding examination.

SECTION 3: APPLICATION PROCEDURE AND REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
3.1 Application Window: Applications open within 3 days of regular result declaration and close after 10 working days.
3.2 Documents to Submit:
(a) Duly filled Supplementary Examination Form signed by the student and Academic Mentor.
(b) Official Grade Sheet copy indicating Grade 'F' for the subject.
(c) Attendance clearance certificate from course instructor confirming attendance >= 75.0%.
(d) Fee payment receipt of Rs. 1000 per registered course paid via the university billing portal.
3.3 Submission Office: Submit hard copies or online verified PDF to the Office of the Controller of Examinations.
