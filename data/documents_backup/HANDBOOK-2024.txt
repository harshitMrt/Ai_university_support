SECTION 2: CAMPUS FACILITIES AND HOURS
2.1 Central Library: Open Monday through Saturday from 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM. During examination periods, the reading rooms remain open until 2:00 AM.
2.2 Health Centre: 24/7 emergency medical care available on south campus. Routine consultations from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
2.3 Sports Complex: Indoor badminton, gym, and swimming pool operate from 6:00 AM to 8:30 PM.

SECTION 5: STUDENT GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
5.1 Grievance Cell: Any unresolved academic or administrative grievances may be escalated to the Student Ombudsman via email: ombudsman@university.edu or at Room 104, Student Welfare Complex.
