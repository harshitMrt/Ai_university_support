SECTION 1: SHORT TITLE AND COMMENCEMENT
1.1 These regulations shall be designated as the Academic Regulations for Undergraduate Engineering Programmes (B.Tech), Version 3.1.
1.2 These regulations come into force with effect from July 1, 2024, and supersede all previous versions including Version 2.0 (ACAD-REG-2021).

SECTION 2: DEFINITIONS AND CURRICULAR STRUCTURE
2.1 'Course' means a unit of study in a semester having defined credits, contact hours, and syllabus.
2.2 'CGPA' refers to Cumulative Grade Point Average calculated on a 10-point scale across all completed semesters.
2.3 'Active Backlog' denotes any registered course in which a student has failed (Grade 'F') or was debarred (Grade 'AD') and has not yet cleared.

SECTION 4: ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENTS
4.1 Minimum Attendance: Every registered student is mandatory required to maintain a minimum attendance of 75.0% in every course (theory, practical, and integrated) to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examinations.
4.2 Calculation: Attendance percentage is strictly computed as: (Total Classes Attended / Total Classes Held) * 100.
4.3 Shortage and Debarment: Any student securing less than 75.0% attendance in a course shall be categorized as 'Attendance Debarred' (Grade 'AD'). Debarred students are strictly prohibited from writing the end-semester examination in that course and shall not be eligible for immediate supplementary examinations.
4.4 Condonation on Medical Grounds: The Dean of Academic Affairs may consider condonation of attendance shortage up to a maximum of 10% (i.e., minimum 65.0% physical attendance required) exclusively on genuine medical hospitalization or severe illness grounds, supported by verified medical records submitted within 7 working days of recovery.

SECTION 7: SUPPLEMENTARY EXAMINATIONS
7.1 Eligibility: A student who has appeared in the regular end-semester examination and secured an 'F' grade is eligible to appear for the supplementary examination.
7.2 Pre-requisite Requirements:
(a) The student must have satisfied the minimum attendance requirement of 75.0% during the regular semester.
(b) The student must not have more than 2 active backlogs across all previous semesters at the time of supplementary exam registration.
7.3 Debarred Ineligibility: Students who were debarred due to attendance shortage (<75.0%) cannot register for supplementary exams and must repeat the course during summer term or regular semester.

SECTION 8: PASSING CRITERIA AND GRADING
8.1 Passing Requirement: To pass a course, a candidate must obtain at least 40.0% marks in aggregate (internal assessment + external end-semester examination combined).
8.2 Failure: A candidate obtaining less than 40.0% total marks shall be awarded an 'F' grade and recorded as a backlog.
