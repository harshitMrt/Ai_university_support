SECTION 1: EXAMINATION TIMELINES AND CONDUCT
1.1 End-semester examinations are conducted twice annually (Autumn Session in December, Spring Session in May).
1.2 Hall Tickets: Only students who have cleared all tuition fees and satisfy the minimum 75.0% attendance threshold shall receive admit cards.

SECTION 5: ASSESSMENT WEIGHTAGE AND PASSING CRITERIA
5.1 Evaluation Components:
- Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA): 40% (Quizzes, assignments, mid-semester test)
- End-Semester Examination: 60%
5.2 Passing Requirement: A student must obtain a minimum of 40.0 marks out of 100 in total (CIA + End-Sem combined). Students obtaining 39.9 marks or lower fail the course with Grade 'F'.
5.3 Grading Scale:
- 90 - 100: Grade O (Outstanding, Grade Point 10)
- 80 - 89: Grade A+ (Excellent, Grade Point 9)
- 70 - 79: Grade A (Very Good, Grade Point 8)
- 60 - 69: Grade B+ (Good, Grade Point 7)
- 50 - 59: Grade B (Above Average, Grade Point 6)
- 40 - 49: Grade C (Pass, Grade Point 5)
- Less than 40: Grade F (Fail, Grade Point 0)
- Debarred due to Attendance: Grade AD (Debarred, Grade Point 0)
