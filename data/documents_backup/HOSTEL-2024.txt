SECTION 1: RESIDENTIAL CODE
1.1 University hostel accommodation is a privilege subject to strict academic performance, discipline, and regular attendance.

SECTION 4: TIMINGS AND CURFEW
4.1 Main Gate Curfew: Residential students must return to the hostel premises by 9:30 PM on Monday through Friday, and 10:00 PM on Saturdays and Sundays.
4.2 Late Entry: Late entry without prior approved warden slip leads to disciplinary warning. Three late entries result in suspension of hostel privileges.

SECTION 6: ALLOTMENT RENEWAL ELIGIBILITY
6.1 Academic Attendance: To qualify for room allotment renewal for the next academic year, the resident must have secured a minimum of 80.0% attendance in the current semester.
6.2 Disciplinary Record: Residents with proven disciplinary misconduct, property damage, or ragging infractions will be evicted permanently.
